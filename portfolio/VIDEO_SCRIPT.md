# AgentOps AI — 55-second Portfolio Demo Storyboard

Target duration: 50–55 seconds.
Format: 4:3 or 16:9 MP4, under 100 MB.
Narration: English, calm and natural.

## 0–7 seconds — Product hook
Visual: open the live dashboard and keep the RAG/evaluation cards visible.

Narration:
"AgentOps AI is a full-stack AI operations demo that combines grounded RAG, tool-using agents, and human approval for sensitive actions."

## 7–18 seconds — Grounded retrieval
Visual: search for "express shipping" and show the Shipping Guide result.

Narration:
"The knowledge layer retrieves tenant-scoped evidence with source names and similarity scores, so agent decisions can be inspected instead of treated like a black box."

## 18–31 seconds — Multi-agent trace
Visual: run "Summarize renewal risk for Acme." Show the completed Knowledge Search and Customer Lookup trace.

Narration:
"For account-risk analysis, the agent plans a short workflow, retrieves policy context, looks up synthetic account indicators, and exposes each step in the execution trace."

## 31–44 seconds — Safety gate
Visual: run "Create a support ticket for Acme." Stop on the yellow Approval required state.

Narration:
"Write actions do not execute automatically. AgentOps pauses at an explicit approval gate and clearly labels the requested write tool."

## 44–51 seconds — Approved result
Visual: click Approve action and show the completed synthetic ticket result.

Narration:
"Only after approval does the demo action complete, preserving a visible audit trail."

## 51–55 seconds — Close
Visual: return attention to the evaluation cards and API Online indicator.

Narration:
"The demo is live, tested, and built to showcase practical RAG, agent orchestration, safety, and full-stack AI engineering."

## Editing notes
- Do not show browser bookmarks, email addresses, account identifiers, or unrelated tabs.
- Do not add logos from third-party companies.
- Keep on-screen text readable and avoid text-heavy title cards.
- Use the real deployed app for every product shot.
- If background music is added, keep it subtle enough that narration stays clear.
